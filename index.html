How it works

01Orbit

A virtual camera moves around the object in equal steps and renders one 224 × 224 frame per step. The dial around the camera view is a top-down map of this orbit.

02Encode

A pretrained vision model turns each frame into one feature vector, L2-normalized. It runs on your machine with transformers.js, so no image leaves the browser.

03Project

PCA shows the three directions of largest variance. The ellipse fit finds the best loop of the form w₁·cos θ + w₂·sin θ, then adds the largest remaining direction as height.

04Score

Ring score (0–100) rates how round and how well-ordered the PCA loop is. Ellipse R² is the share of embedding variance that a pure loop in the camera angle explains.
